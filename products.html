The ledger, the budget, the report — one product, three views

Stackly is one platform that shows the same data three ways: a running ledger, a budget you can watch, and a report you can actually read.

01 — The Ledger

Every transaction, sorted the moment it happens

Connected accounts sync continuously. New charges appear categorised, merchant-matched, and searchable within a minute — no manual tagging required.

  • Search any transaction by merchant, amount, or category
  • Split a single charge across multiple categories
  • Attach a note or receipt photo to any line
02 — Budgets

Set a limit, and get warned before you cross it

Budgets in Stackly aren't static — they compare your current pace against your own history and flag categories that are trending over, days before the month ends.

03 — Receipt capture

Photograph it once, match it forever

Snap a paper receipt and Stackly reads the merchant and total, then matches it to the right card transaction automatically — proof of purchase, filed without effort.

  • Works from your phone camera or a forwarded email
  • Flags receipts that don't match any transaction
  • Exportable as a zip for accounting or reimbursement
04 — Reports

A monthly summary you'll actually finish reading

Instead of a wall of charts, Stackly writes two or three sentences explaining what changed and why, backed by the numbers if you want to dig in.

"Your dining spend rose 18% this month, driven mostly by three weekends out. Everything else tracked close to your usual pace."

05 — Security

Read-only access, encrypted everywhere

Stackly never has the ability to move your money. Every connection is read-only, every credential is encrypted at rest, and you can revoke access to any account in one tap.

Bank-grade encryption

AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit — the same standard used by the banks you connect.

Read-only, always

Stackly can see your transactions but can never initiate a transfer, payment, or withdrawal.

Revoke anytime

Disconnect any linked account instantly from your settings — no calls, no waiting period.
