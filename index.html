What it does

Everything a ledger should do, minus the manual entry

Connect your accounts once. Stackly reads every transaction, sorts it, and tells you what changed — so your job is deciding, not data entry.

Automatic categorisation

Every transaction is sorted into food, transport, bills, and more within seconds of syncing — and you can retrain any category with one click.

Budgets that adjust

Set a monthly limit per category and Stackly warns you three days before you'd normally overshoot, based on your own past pace.

Receipt capture

Photograph a paper receipt and Stackly matches it to the right card transaction automatically, keeping proof for every rupee spent.

Reports that explain

Monthly summaries written in plain language — what went up, what went down, and why — not just charts you have to interpret yourself.

Shared ledgers

Split a household or team budget with collaborators, each seeing exactly the categories they're responsible for.

Bank-grade security

256-bit encryption, read-only bank connections, and two-factor login — your data is visible to you and no one else.
