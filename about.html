Money is easier to manage when it's easy to see.

Stackly Expense started as a shared spreadsheet between three friends who kept guessing where their salaries went. It's now a ledger platform for thousands of people who wanted the same honesty, without the formulas.

Our story

Built by people who were tired of finding out too late

In 2022, our three co-founders were splitting an apartment and constantly disagreed about who owed what. Every finance app they tried either oversimplified spending into three colours, or demanded so much manual entry that nobody kept it up.

So they built a ledger that read transactions automatically and explained them in plain sentences. Friends asked to use it. Then friends' employers did. Stackly Expense is that same ledger, grown up.

What we believe

Three principles that shape every feature

Clarity before automation

We automate categorisation so you can see faster, not so you stop looking. Every number stays one tap away from its source transaction.

Your data stays yours

Read-only bank access, encryption at rest, and no selling of transaction data — ever. We built this because we didn't trust the alternative either.

Money is a shared subject

Households and small teams need the same visibility individuals do, without giving up their own privacy. Ledgers are built to be shared safely.

How it works

A ledger that keeps itself, mostly

Stackly Expense connects to your accounts read-only, so it can see transactions the moment they post without ever moving money on your behalf. From there, the categorisation engine does the tedious part, and you stay in charge of the judgment calls.

01

Connect an account

Link a bank, card, or wallet in read-only mode. Nothing is ever moved or authorised from Stackly.

02

Let it read the ledger

Transactions are categorised automatically and explained in plain language, not just a merchant code.

03

Review and share

Correct a category in one tap, set a budget, or invite a household member into the same ledger.
